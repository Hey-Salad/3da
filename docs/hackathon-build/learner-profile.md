## Project Idea

- Initial idea: 3DA, an open-source, local-first operating layer for people and agents sharing one or more 3D printers. Users can discover capacity, reserve a printer, validate and prepare a job, explicitly approve consequential actions, submit a print through a local bridge, monitor it, and recover safely from failures.
- Initial verified hardware: Two printers in the participant's home fleet, with Bambu Lab A1 as the first named integration target.
- Primary users: Makers, families, coworking-space members, schools and labs, fabrication-room managers, and fleet operators.
- Central demo moment: Codex discovers the room, reserves Samantha, validates the job, requests human approval, and starts a real physical print while every WebMCP action appears in 3DA's live audit timeline.
- Demo object: A physical OpenAI token.
- Product boundary: Shared-space orchestration and safe WebMCP control are central; general AI-generated CAD is not.
